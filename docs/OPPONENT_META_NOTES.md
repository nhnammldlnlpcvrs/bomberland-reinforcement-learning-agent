# Opponent Meta Notes

## Baseline Agents

Baselines are useful for sanity checks but do not fully represent online meta. Track whether the agent wins, draws, or survives without converting.

## Online Leaderboard Agents

Hidden agents may be more aggressive, more defensive, or better at trapping than local baselines. Online results need enough games before conclusions.

## Styles to Watch

- Aggressive bots: rush and bomb often.
- Defensive bots: survive and force long games.
- Box farmers: gather items before fighting.
- Territory controllers: occupy center/open space and deny escape paths.
- Anti-camping behavior: punishes spawn loops and passive corners.

## What to Observe in Replay

Watch enemy distance, bomb ownership, corridor entries, whether our agent opens boxes, and whether it keeps safe space after bombing.
